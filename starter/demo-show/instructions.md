## Channels
- Walk-in music ("Music") on Aux 1, stereo, source LCL 3-4. Fader at -20dB.

## Busses
- "Vox Wedge": Lead Vox's wedge. Lead Vox sends to it. Patch to AES50-A output 1.
- "Gtr Wedge": the stage-left wedge. Gtr 1 and BV Gtr send to it. Patch to AES50-A output 2.
- "Keys Wedge": the stage-right wedge. Keys and BV Keys send to it. Patch to AES50-A output 3.
- "Drum IEM": the drummer's in-ears. Kick, Snare, Bass DI, Lead Vox and Playback send to it. Patch to AES50-A outputs 7 and 8.
